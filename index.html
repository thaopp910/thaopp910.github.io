Content Hub

A private, single-user tool. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

What it does

Content Hub helps its owner plan and review their own content work. With the owner's permission, it reads aggregated reports from two Google services the owner already has access to:

  • Google Analytics (read-only): sessions, traffic sources and page views.
  • Google Search Console (read-only): clicks, impressions and search queries.

It never changes anything in those accounts, and it does not access any other Google data.
